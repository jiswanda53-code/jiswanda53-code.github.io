My Story

"If I'm not suited for engineering, business, or athletics… what am I capable of?"

I kept failing at automotive engineering, small businesses, and trading - each attempt closing a door. My mother told me I didn't have to force myself into something uncomfortable. Looking at my hobbies and my long-standing fascination with what happens "behind the screen," I found cybersecurity: a field where failure isn't a dead end, it's part of the process.

"I didn't need to find something that came naturally.
I needed something worth learning."
